The dissertation examines whether machine-learning models can be reduced to simpler structures without giving up the aspects of their performance that matter in practice. It connects two forms of simplification: selecting and sign-inverting weights in neural networks, and concentrating random-forest forecast distributions on a smaller set of supporting observations.
